What survives AI is process
Four steps. Any message.
Any channel.

In the next ten minutes you'll be able to run S.T.E.P. on any suspicious contact (email, text, phone, or in person) and teach it to someone else.
Step 1 of 4
STOP
Don't act inside the message.

Hands off the link. Don't answer the ask. The message is engineered to be completed on reflex. The pause is the intervention.

Step 2 of 4
THINK
Name two things:
What is it asking me to do?
What is it making me feel?

Urgency. Fear. Authority. Curiosity. Duty. Pressure is data. The feeling is the fingerprint of manipulation.

Step 3 of 4
EXAMINE
Check what you can verify.

Email → the sender's real domain, the link's real destination.
Phone → can they prove who they are on a channel you control?

Two or three checks per channel. The writing can mislead. The infrastructure can be checked.

Step 4 of 4
PROCEED
Always end in an action.

Suspicious → check through a trusted channel and report.
Clean → carry on with your day.

The process never leaves you frozen, and it protects your time, too.

Findings

Sender: unexpected variationnorthstar-health.org ≠ northstarhealth.org. The display name looks familiar. The domain is a lookalike.
Two levers, pulling toward fastDeadline pressure plus helpfulness: "don't hold up the team." That feeling is the fingerprint.
The link's real destinationHover, don't click. An unfamiliar domain. The writing may look polished, but the infrastructure is still inspectable.

Findings

Caller ID is spoofableThe name on the screen proves very little. Identity can be faked. Channel control is harder to fake.
Their number, their windowA 20-minute deadline at 7:42 AM is engineered for pre-shift autopilot. Call back on the published help desk line, a channel you control.
Never share a verification codeLegitimate support staff should not ask you to read back a sign-in code. That code may approve access to your account.
The sixth lever: duty"Before it affects patient care" weaponizes your values. In healthcare, that's the pressure signal.

Why it passes

Expected organization domainThe address ends in northstarhealth.org, with no unfamiliar variation or lookalike.
It gives you an outA deadline, but no threat. Legit messages often offer an exit. Attacks usually try to keep you inside their path.
It offers other channelsThe portal "as usual," a phone extension. Safer messages give you alternatives to the link in front of you.
