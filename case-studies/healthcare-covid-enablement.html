Case Study

Healthcare Training Under COVID Constraints

A public-safe summary of rapid digital-first healthcare training created when in-person training was not possible and frontline workers needed critical systems and safety preparation.

OrganizationLegacy Health
IndustryHealthcare
Company size12,000+ employee health system
Focus areasDigital-first training, healthcare enablement, knowledge systems

Organizational Context

COVID disrupted classroom-based training at a moment when incoming frontline workers needed rapid preparation for critical systems, infection control, and safety procedures.

Environmental Factors

  • High-pressure healthcare environment.
  • Urgent need for digital-first delivery.
  • Operational constraints and rapidly changing conditions.

The Challenge

The organization needed critical training to move quickly into digital formats while preserving clarity, usability, and relevance for frontline healthcare work.

Key Friction Points

  • Classroom delivery was not viable.
  • Frontline workers needed practical systems and safety training quickly.
  • Training had to support high-stakes healthcare workflows.

Strategy & Systems Design

The approach was rapid digital enablement: translating urgent healthcare training needs into accessible online learning and support resources.

Systems Built

  • Digital-first Epic EHR training.
  • Infection control and safety procedure training.
  • IS knowledge base redesign for self-service support.

Conceptual System View

PressureUrgent healthcare training needs
→
AccessDigital-first learning and support
→
UseWorkflow guidance and self-service resources
→
ContinuityOperational readiness under constraints

Why This Approach

Under COVID constraints, the immediate priority was operational continuity and safe readiness. Digital-first training and self-service support were necessary because traditional delivery channels were unavailable.

Implementation & Rollout

The work supported a 12,000+ employee health system and required rapid development of digital learning for urgent operational needs.

Content / Asset Types

  • Digital training modules.
  • Healthcare workflow support materials.
  • Knowledge base content and self-service support resources.

Outcomes & Signals

Quantitative Signals

  • Supported a 12,000+ employee health system.
  • IS knowledge base used by approximately 1,500 staff.

Qualitative Outcomes

  • Enabled digital-first training during COVID constraints.
  • Helped incoming frontline workers access critical systems and safety preparation.
  • Knowledge base redesign produced a measurable reduction in support tickets.

Lessons Learned

In high-pressure environments, learning systems have to prioritize clarity, speed, and operational usefulness. The best solution is not always the most polished artifact; it is the one that helps people do critical work safely and quickly.
