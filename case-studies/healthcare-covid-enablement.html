Case Study · Healthcare Enablement

Healthcare Application Training Under COVID Constraints

How I translated changing healthcare systems, safety requirements, and workflows into digital learning and performance support for a 12,000+ employee health system.

Legacy Health
Period

Delivery
eLearning, job aids, quick references, and knowledge systems

Organizational Context

COVID removed classroom delivery at the same time incoming frontline workers needed fast, accurate preparation for critical systems, infection control, safety procedures, and changing operational expectations. The work sat inside a regional healthcare environment where information had to be understandable, current, and usable during real work.

My Role and Scope

As the sole instructional designer supporting the System Office, I worked across healthcare, information services, and operational teams. I translated Epic-enabled workflows and changing safety requirements into eLearning, job aids, quick references, onboarding materials, and performance support.

My responsibility extended beyond producing content. I helped clarify objectives, document the workflow to be taught, coordinate subject-matter review, prepare materials for delivery, and maintain learner access through Workday Learning and the enterprise LMS.

The Challenge

The organization needed to move essential learning into digital formats quickly without losing the context people needed to perform high-stakes work. Three tensions shaped the solution:

  • Speed and accuracy: guidance changed quickly, but content still required reliable source material and expert validation.
  • Coverage and usability: a 12,000+ employee system needed scalable delivery, while each asset still had to be practical at the moment of need.
  • Training and support: formal learning could introduce a process, but staff also needed searchable, self-service resources after the course.

Discovery and Validation

I partnered with healthcare, IS, and operational subject-matter experts to identify the required behavior, map the relevant workflow, separate essential actions from background information, and validate the accuracy of each deliverable. Review cycles were especially important when system steps or safety guidance changed.

This created a shared definition of readiness: learners needed to know what to do, where to do it, and where to find help later.

Design Decisions

  • Design around work: organize content by the decisions and actions staff needed to complete, not by the structure of the source material.
  • Use the lightest effective format: reserve modules for concepts and guided practice; use job aids and quick references for repeatable workflow support.
  • Build for revision: use consistent templates, version control, and review cycles so updates could move through the system without rebuilding every asset.
  • Connect learning to self-service: strengthen the knowledge base so formal training was supported by searchable answers after delivery.

Implementation & Rollout

Deliverables were published and maintained through Workday Learning and the enterprise LMS, supporting review cycles, version control, delivery readiness, and learner access. Instructor-facing needs and reusable presentation structures were addressed alongside learner-facing assets so the delivery system remained coherent.

In parallel, I redesigned an information-services knowledge base used by approximately 1,500 staff. This extended the learning experience beyond assigned training and gave employees a clearer route to answers during work.

Outcomes & Signals

  • Supported a 12,000+ employee health system.
  • Enabled digital-first preparation for Epic-enabled workflows, infection control, safety, onboarding, compliance, and operational needs.
  • Extended support through an IS knowledge base used by approximately 1,500 staff.
  • Knowledge base redesign produced a measurable reduction in support tickets.

What This Demonstrates

This work shows how I approach software and system application training as a connected service: understand the workflow, align stakeholders on the source of truth, choose the right learning and support formats, prepare delivery, and maintain access as the system changes.

It also reinforced a principle I still use: in a high-pressure environment, the strongest learning solution is the one people can trust and use at the exact moment their work demands it.
