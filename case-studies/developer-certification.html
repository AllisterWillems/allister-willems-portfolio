Case Study

Developer Certification & Technical Enablement

A public-safe summary of technical enablement work designed to make platform development, analytics tools, and internal product processes easier to learn and scale.

OrganizationRoku
IndustryMedia technology / streaming
AudienceDevelopers and cross-functional product audiences
Focus areasDeveloper education, certification, product analytics, technical training

Organizational Context

Technical audiences needed clearer pathways into platform development and internal tools. Cross-functional product teams also needed practical support for analytics, experimentation, and development processes.

Environmental Factors

  • Complex internal platform and product workflows.
  • Need for structured developer readiness.
  • Need for cross-functional understanding of analytics and experimentation tools.

The Challenge

The work required converting technical and process-heavy knowledge into structured learning that could support onboarding, assessment, and ongoing enablement.

Key Friction Points

  • Developer onboarding and platform education needed more structure.
  • Analytics tools required shared terminology and practical workflows.
  • SME knowledge had to become clear enough for broader audiences.

Strategy & Systems Design

The approach was to design technical enablement systems that could scale beyond one-off explanations or SME-dependent support.

Systems Built

  • A multi-module Developer Certification Program with assessments.
  • Product analytics enablement for Amoeba and Looker.
  • Training around development processes, feature planning, tools, and cross-functional responsibilities.

Conceptual System View

KnowledgePlatform, process, and product context
→
StructureSequenced curriculum and SME input
→
ValidationAssessment and certification model
→
ReadinessMore consistent technical capability

Why This Approach

Certification, assessment, and structured technical learning were better suited than informal documentation alone because the goal was readiness, not just reference access.

Implementation & Rollout

The Developer Certification Program was designed as a multi-module curriculum. Related technical enablement work translated analytics and development processes into practical learning for product and technical audiences.

Content / Asset Types

  • Certification curriculum.
  • Assessments.
  • Technical training modules.
  • Process and tool guidance.
  • SME-informed learning materials.

Outcomes & Signals

Quantitative Signals

  • Exact developer participation counts are not available for this public V1.

Qualitative Outcomes

  • The Developer Certification Program remained active as of departure.
  • Technical knowledge became more structured, assessable, and easier to reuse.
  • Product and technical audiences gained clearer pathways into platform, analytics, and experimentation workflows.

Lessons Learned

Technical enablement is strongest when it combines SME knowledge with clear learner pathways, assessment, and practical application. Reference material alone rarely creates readiness.
