Enterprise AI

Enterprise AI Learning Ecosystem

Problem
Employees needed practical AI fluency as tools and workflows evolved faster than formal learning structures.
What I built
Company-wide AI literacy and enablement covering LLMs, prompting, RAG, MCP, agents, Copilot, Claude, Glean, and AI-assisted workflows.
Why it mattered
The work helped close non-technical AI fluency gaps and framed AI adoption as workflow capability, not tool exposure.
Skills demonstrated
AI enablement, learning strategy, workflow adoption, responsible AI communication, technical translation.

Behavior Change

Cybersecurity Behavior Change

Problem
Security training needed to move beyond passive awareness toward employee vigilance and reporting behavior.
What I built
Cybersecurity and safety training systems, including a 10-module Gatekeeper Security Training suite built with specialist SMEs.
Why it mattered
The work contributed to increased threat reporting, a 600+ attendee launch webcast, and campaign engagement around 40% open rates.
Skills demonstrated
Risk communication, behavior change, scenario design, enterprise rollout, SME collaboration.
Leadership Systems

Manager Capability & Leadership Enablement

Problem
Leadership expectations needed to become observable manager behaviors, not abstract values or one-time training events.
What I built
Manager capability architecture using behavior clusters, playbooks, micro-skills, practice loops, anti-patterns, and adoption indicators.
Why it mattered
The system connected leadership development to real work, including feedback, psychological safety, accountability, and friction removal.
Skills demonstrated
Leadership enablement, behavior design, manager reinforcement, facilitation architecture, organizational capability.
Healthcare Enablement

Healthcare Training Under COVID Constraints

What I built

Why it mattered
Critical healthcare learning moved quickly into digital delivery under operational pressure.
Skills demonstrated
Healthcare learning, rapid digital transformation, performance support, knowledge systems, high-stakes enablement.

About
