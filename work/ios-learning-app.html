Wildlife Learning App for iOS

A native iOS learning app designed and built end-to-end in Swift and SwiftUI as a personal skill demonstration. The app teaches learners about wildlife species through self-contained learning modules — each combining narrative descriptions, swipeable photo galleries, embedded video, and an interactive habitat range map rendered on a globe.

The project brings together the two halves of my background: learning design and native interaction design. Content is structured the way good learning content should be — concise explanations, layered media, and spatial context — while the interface uses native iOS patterns like tab-based navigation, card layouts, and inline media players so the experience feels immediately familiar to learners.

Building learning products in code, rather than in authoring tools, means the experience can be exactly what the content needs: custom galleries, integrated video, and interactive geography in a single continuous flow.
