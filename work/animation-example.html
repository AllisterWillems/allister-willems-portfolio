Visual Work · 2026

Animation

As part of Roku's legal and compliance education program, I partnered with Legal and Compliance subject matter experts to develop a series of animated learning experiences that prepared employees for unexpected regulatory inspections, commonly referred to as “Dawn Raids.”

Because these situations are uncommon but carry significant legal and business implications, animation provided an effective way to recreate realistic scenarios in a safe learning environment. The series focused on helping employees understand their responsibilities, respond appropriately under pressure, and recognize when to involve Legal and Corporate Security. By combining storytelling, character animation, and scenario-based instruction, the learning experience transformed complex legal guidance into clear, memorable actions employees could confidently apply if the situation ever occurred.
