Creative Storytelling Through Video

At Blurb, I directed and produced a series of documentary-style films featuring photographers, designers, illustrators, and authors who used Blurb's self-publishing platform to share their creative work with the world.

Rather than creating traditional promotional videos, these films focused on authentic storytelling. Each project explored the creative process behind the artist's work, the motivations that inspired them, and the role publishing played in bringing their ideas to life. From identifying compelling stories and conducting interviews to directing production, cinematography, editing, color grading, and motion graphics, I managed the creative process from concept through final delivery. The result was a collection of films that celebrated creativity while strengthening Blurb's connection with its creative community through genuine, human-centered storytelling.
