Roku Physical Security Training

At Roku, I partnered with Physical Security and Corporate Security subject matter experts to design a comprehensive employee safety and security education program that helped employees recognize potential risks, respond appropriately, and contribute to a safer workplace.

Rather than treating physical security as a collection of policies, the learning experience emphasized situational awareness, practical decision-making, and individual responsibility. Topics included visitor management, recognizing suspicious behavior, handling suspicious mail and packages, workplace violence prevention, travel safety, demonstrations and civil unrest, and social engineering. The program combined instructional strategy, visual communication, multimedia production, and scenario-based learning to make security concepts practical, memorable, and relevant to everyday work.
