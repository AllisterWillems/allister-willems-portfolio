Visual Work · 2024

Instructional Video & Multimedia Production

Throughout my career, I've used video as a learning tool to help people understand complex concepts, develop practical skills, and build confidence in new ways of working. Across organizations including Blurb, GE Digital, Legacy Health, and Roku, I've partnered with subject matter experts to create instructional videos that make technical, procedural, and conceptual information more approachable and actionable.

Rather than approaching video as a standalone medium, I view it as one component of a broader learning system. Each production begins with understanding the learner, the performance challenge, and the behaviors the organization hopes to influence. From there, I develop the instructional strategy, visual narrative, and production approach that best supports the learning objectives. Depending on the project, I serve as director, producer, cinematographer, editor, motion graphics designer, and instructional designer, managing the creative process from concept through final delivery.

The examples below represent instructional videos created across healthcare, enterprise technology, customer education, cybersecurity, and AI enablement initiatives. Together, they demonstrate how thoughtful multimedia production can help translate complex expertise into engaging learning experiences that support real-world performance.

By embracing these multifaceted roles across different organizations, I have honed a diverse skill set to oversee multimedia projects from concept to completion. My experience in directing, cinematography, editing, and motion graphics design enables me to create impactful learning experiences that resonate with audiences and drive meaningful engagement.
